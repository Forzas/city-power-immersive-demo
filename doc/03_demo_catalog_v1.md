# Urban Systems Education Project | Demo Catalog v1

## First-Priority Demos

### 1. Urban Power Grid
- Introduction: How a kilowatt-hour travels from a power plant to an outlet at home.
- Tags: Infrastructure / Energy / Process Visualization
- Interaction: Clickable nodes and animated electricity flow

### 2. Urban Water Supply System
- Introduction: The journey of a drop of water from its source to a household faucet and back to the environment.
- Tags: Infrastructure / Water Cycle / Pipe Network
- Interaction: Process switching and cross-sectional views

### 3. The Journey After Waste Sorting
- Introduction: Waste does not disappear after it is discarded; it enters a new urban process.
- Tags: Sustainability / Recycling / Urban Operations
- Interaction: Routing choices and treatment-process animation

### 4. The Life of a Package
- Introduction: How a package crosses sorting centers, long-haul transport, and last-mile delivery to reach you.
- Tags: Logistics / Everyday Life / Dispatching
- Interaction: Animated map and timeline

### 5. A Day in Metro Operations
- Introduction: How a metro line maintains stable service through peak and off-peak periods.
- Tags: Transportation / Dispatching / Passenger Flow
- Interaction: State switching and an operations-control view

### 6. How a Building Works
- Introduction: How electricity, water, HVAC, fire protection, elevators, and networks support a building together.
- Tags: Buildings / System Coordination / Cross-Section
- Interaction: Building cross-section and floor-by-floor exploration
